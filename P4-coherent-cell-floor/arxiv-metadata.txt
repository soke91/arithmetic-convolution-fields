TITLE
-----
Cell-mean fluctuations in an arithmetic convolution field: exact
Rademacher-null variances and a permutation control


PRIMARY CATEGORY
----------------
math.NT

The contribution is arithmetic structure, an exactly computable null
covariance, and a singular-series mechanism -- not a statistical
method, so a statistics category would reach the wrong readers.
math.PR is a possible cross-list.


ABSTRACT (plain text)
---------------------
Let Z(N) = C(N)/sqrt(V(N)) with C(N) = sum_{n<N} Lambda(n) mu(N-n) and
V(N) = sum_{v<N} mu^2(v) Lambda(N-v)^2, and partition a band of even N
into cells indexed by how many of 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 divide N. Asking
whether a cell mean departs from the band mean needs a reference scale
for that contrast.

We compute the scale exactly. Under the null replacing the mu(v) on
their own support by independent Rademacher signs, the variance of any
cell contrast is a doubly centred quadratic form in the field's
covariance kernel and reduces to three convolutions. That sd/sqrt(n_c)
fails for correlated observations is standard; what is not is that here
the kernel is exactly computable, that divisibility cells align with
it, and that a uniformly random cell of the same size recovers the
finite-population formula exactly -- so the count-based scale is right
for a random cell and wrong for these. Measured over eight octaves to
1.6 x 10^7, the exact null standard deviation shrinks by 1.21 where a
count predicts 11.3, and a label permutation preserving every cell size
collapses it by factors 3.8 to 105.

For its size we offer a mechanism: the excess of the shift's singular
series over same-cell pairs, whose main term is rational and carries no
fitted parameter. On the partition that motivated it the agreement is
post hoc and one rule registered there was refuted. The mechanism was
then written down for a second, differently shaped partition and its
predictions committed before that partition was measured; the exact
measurement falls inside the registered interval at every cell. That
transfer, not the fit on the first partition, is the evidence offered.

No distributional law for Z is asserted, no Goldbach consequence is
drawn, and the decay of the effect with N is not determined here.

SUBMISSION FACTS
----------------
Endorsement is required for a first submission to a category. One
positive endorsement from a qualified author in that category suffices,
and endorsers judge whether the submitter works in the field rather
than whether the paper is correct.

The automatic route runs on an institutional email address associated
with the account together with claimed co-authorship. The author of
this paper is unaffiliated, so that route does not apply and a math.NT
endorser has to be found. arXiv counts as qualified an author with
enough papers in the category submitted between three months and five
years earlier.

This is the one obstacle on the arXiv side that the manuscript cannot
remove, and it is unrelated to the manuscript.

arXiv requires text-to-text generative AI use to be reported consistent
with subject standards, and holds each named author individually
responsible for all contents however generated. The paper carries that
disclosure in its appendix.

The source uses amsmath, amssymb, amsthm, geometry and hyperref only,
so it builds on arXiv's TeX without external style files.

A submitted version is not removable: later versions replace it in
display but v1 remains, and a withdrawal leaves a tombstone.
