the null type did the work. An iid coin at full resolution still
excludes mu where a multiplicative ensemble does not, so iid signs
are narrower than the right null when the object is multiplicative.
Every figure in this repository calibrated against a coin was
calibrated against something narrower than it should have been. This
is one band and one statistic and it says the question is worth
asking of lem:coin in general, not that the answer is known.
