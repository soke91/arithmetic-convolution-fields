  DIAGNOSTIC 1 (post hoc). Where the failures come from. For N
  with several small odd prime factors every term of E_3 has the
  same sign -- |E_3| = B exactly -- so there is no cancellation
  across k at all. The reason is visible in the smallest
  admissible k: k must be coprime to N, and by [eq:dilate] the m
  that survive are coprime to rad(N) too, so over a short range
  they are almost all primes and mu(m) = -1 dominates.
