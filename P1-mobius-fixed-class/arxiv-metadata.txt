TITLE
-----
The Huang-Li reduction of Goldbach to Elliott-Halberstam does not
decompose, with an unconditional bound for a Moebius-weighted
correlation sum in fixed residue classes


PRIMARY CATEGORY
----------------
math.NT


ABSTRACT (plain text)
---------------------
Huang and Li reduce binary Goldbach for large even N to a
Moebius-twisted Elliott-Halberstam hypothesis EH_mu(N^{theta'}) with a
single theta' > 1/2, consumed at two places in their Section 3, the
functionals E_4(alpha) and E_3(alpha). We show that this reduction does
not decompose: neither consumption has a half whose discharge would be
partial progress. One is unnecessary -- the Moebius-weighted
correlation sum underlying E_4 is O_A(N (log N)^{-A}) for every A > 0,
unconditionally and uniformly in the truncation point -- so that
section's demand collapses to the scalar E_3(alpha). The other is not
an equidistribution statement to be established separately: by an
unconditional identity it is Huang-Li's own equation (22), which
already yields binary Goldbach for large even N, and r(N) ~ S(N) N
exactly when sum_{n<N} Lambda(n) mu(N-n) = o(N). The root cause is
mu * log = Lambda: the weight log k carries the Goldbach content, so
every divisor switch must hand it back. The defect in Huang-Li's
equation (18) is not ours: S. Zheleznov reported it earlier and R. Moya
(Zenodo, August 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21939906) exhibits and repairs
it; we add a bound on the missing term. The finite rearrangements are
formalised in Lean 4 against Mathlib over arbitrary functions, so what
is checked is that they use no arithmetic property of Lambda or mu. The
theorem removes only the part of the demand that carries no Goldbach
content, and the net progress toward the Goldbach conjecture is zero.
