TITLE
-----
Cell-mean fluctuations in an arithmetic convolution field: exact
Rademacher-null variances and a permutation control


PRIMARY CATEGORY
----------------
math.NT

The contribution is arithmetic structure, an exactly computable null
covariance, and a singular-series mechanism -- not a statistical
method, so a statistics category would reach the wrong readers.
math.PR is a possible cross-list.


ABSTRACT (plain text)
---------------------
Let Z(N) = C(N)/sqrt(V(N)) with C(N) = sum_{n<N} Lambda(n) mu(N-n) and
V(N) = sum_{v<N} mu^2(v) Lambda(N-v)^2, and partition a band of even N
into cells indexed by how many of 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 divide N. Whether a
cell mean departs from the band mean cannot be asked without a
reference scale for that contrast, and the obvious one -- the sample
deviation over the square root of the cell size -- turns out to be
wrong here by a factor that grows with the band.

Trying to measure that discrepancy produced an exact statement rather
than an estimate. Under the null replacing the mu(v) on their own
support by independent Rademacher signs, the variance of any cell
contrast is a doubly centred quadratic form in the field's covariance
kernel and reduces to three convolutions; a uniformly random cell of
the same size recovers the finite-population formula exactly. That
sd/sqrt(n_c) fails for correlated observations is standard. What is not
is that here the kernel is computable in closed form, so the correct
scale can be written down rather than estimated, and that the
divisibility cells align with it while a random cell does not.

Measured over eight octaves to 1.6 x 10^7, the exact null standard
deviation shrinks by 1.21 where a count predicts 11.3, and a label
permutation preserving every cell size collapses it by factors 3.8 to
105. For its size we offer a candidate descriptor found the same way:
the excess D_c of the shift's singular series over same-cell pairs,
whose main term is a rational number and carries no fitted parameter.
Two things are claimed for it and they carry different weight. That the
residue structure determines D_c was written down for a second,
differently shaped partition and its predictions committed before that
partition was measured; the exact measurement falls inside the
registered interval at every cell, and that transfer is the evidence
offered. That D_c in turn determines the sign-null floor rests on a
correlation of 0.9805 across the six cells of the first partition,
computed after the fact and not transferred: the second partition was
not measured for its floor. Descriptor and mechanism are therefore not
on the same footing here, and the second word is not used for the
second link. One rule registered on the first partition was refuted.

Every registered rule is reported with its verdict, including the
thirteen the runs did not meet and the one recorded void. No
distributional law for Z is asserted, no Goldbach consequence is drawn,
and the decay of the effect with N is not determined here.

SUBMISSION FACTS
----------------
Endorsement is required for a first submission to a category. One
positive endorsement from a qualified author in that category suffices,
and endorsers judge whether the submitter works in the field rather
than whether the paper is correct.

The automatic route runs on an institutional email address associated
with the account together with claimed co-authorship. The author of
this paper is unaffiliated, so that route does not apply and a math.NT
endorser has to be found. arXiv counts as qualified an author with
enough papers in the category submitted between three months and five
years earlier.

This is the one obstacle on the arXiv side that the manuscript cannot
remove, and it is unrelated to the manuscript.

with subject standards, and holds each named author individually
responsible for all contents however generated. The paper carries that
disclosure in its appendix.
